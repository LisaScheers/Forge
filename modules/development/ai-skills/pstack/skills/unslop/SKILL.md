# Unslop

Improve the prose without changing its facts, required structure, intended audience, or level of certainty.

## Edit

- Lead with the actual result or point.
- Replace filler, puffery, vague attribution, and promotional language with concrete facts.
- Use plain words and active voice when they make the sentence clearer.
- Remove generic praise, canned reassurance, process narration, and unnecessary sign-offs.
- Keep one name for each concept instead of cycling through synonyms.
- Split sentences that make the reader backtrack. Vary sentence length naturally.
- Use sentence-case headings and minimal formatting. Avoid decorative emoji and em dashes.
- Preserve material caveats. Do not turn uncertainty into confidence or add claims that the source does not support.

## Check

Ask what each sentence tells the reader to know or do. Cut a sentence that has no specific answer. Keep examples and personality when they help this audience understand the point.

Return the revised artifact, not a report about the edit. Stop when another pass would change only personal taste.
